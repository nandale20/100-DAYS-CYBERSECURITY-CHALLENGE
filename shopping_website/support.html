Get help with your orders, shipping, returns, and more. We're here to assist you with any questions or concerns you may have about your shopping experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our services and policies.

How can I track my order?
You can track your order by logging into your NandaKart account and visiting the "Order History" section. Alternatively, use the tracking link sent to your email or visit our Track Order page. You'll receive real-time updates on your package's location and estimated delivery time.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Products must be unused, in original packaging, and with all tags attached. Some items like intimate apparel, personalized products, and digital downloads may not be eligible for return. Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days after we receive your return.
How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping delivers in 2-3 business days. International shipping times vary by destination and typically take 7-14 business days. Please note that processing time (1-2 business days) is added to these delivery estimates.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination. You can see the exact shipping cost at checkout before placing your order. Please note that customs duties and taxes may apply for international orders and are the responsibility of the recipient.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers for certain orders. All transactions are securely processed with SSL encryption to protect your payment information.

Shipping Information

We offer multiple shipping options to suit your needs. All domestic orders are shipped with tracking information so you can monitor your package every step of the way.

1

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 24-48 hours after payment confirmation. You'll receive an email confirmation with your order details.

2

Packaging & Dispatch

Your items are carefully packaged and dispatched from our warehouse. You'll receive a shipping confirmation with tracking information.

3

Delivery

Your order is delivered to your specified address. For most destinations, you can track your package in real-time using the tracking link.

Returns & Refunds

Our hassle-free return policy ensures you can shop with confidence. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

Return Policy Highlights

  • 30-day return window from delivery date
  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in original packaging
  • Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items
  • Refunds processed within 5-10 business days after we receive your return
  • Exchanges available for size or color changes (subject to availability)
1

Initiate Return

Log into your account, go to "Order History", select the item you want to return, and follow the instructions.

2

Pack & Ship

Pack the item securely in its original packaging with all tags attached. Use the prepaid return label provided.

3

Refund Processed

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.
